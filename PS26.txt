Using KEIL microVision for the LPC2148 ARM7TDMI-S 
microcontroller, write and execute an ARM Assembly 
language program to add two 32-bit numbers and observe 
the operands and result in the general-purpose registers.

#CODE 

    AREA RESET, CODE, READONLY
    ENTRY
    CODE32

START
    LDR R0, =0x12345678
    LDR R1, =0x11111111
    ADD R2, R0, R1

STOP
    B STOP

    END
