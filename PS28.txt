Using KEIL microVision for the LPC2148 ARM7TDMI-S microcontroller, write an ARM Assembly language program to 
perform AND, OR, and XOR operations on two 32-bit values and observe the results in separate registers.

#CODE


        AREA    RESET, CODE, READONLY
        ENTRY
        CODE32

START
        LDR     R0, =0x12345678
        LDR     R1, =0x0F0F0F0F

        AND     R2, R0, R1
        ORR     R3, R0, R1
        EOR     R4, R0, R1

STOP
        B       STOP

        END
