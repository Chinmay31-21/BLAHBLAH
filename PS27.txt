Using KEIL microVision for the LPC2148 ARM7TDMI-S microcontroller, 
write an ARM Assembly language program to subtract two 32-bit 
numbers and observe the result and relevant CPSR flags.

#CODE


        AREA    RESET, CODE, READONLY
        ENTRY
        CODE32

START
        LDR     R0, =0x12345678
        LDR     R1, =0x11111111

        SUBS    R2, R0, R1

        MRS     R3, CPSR

STOP
        B       STOP

        END
